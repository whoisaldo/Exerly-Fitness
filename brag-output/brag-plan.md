# Exerly phone logging film

Invocation: `/brag` for the web landing page, clarified to focus on logging on the phone. Polished landscape video, 1920×1080, 21 seconds, music and sparse SFX, no narration.

## Planning rubric

1. Exerly records food, activity, sleep, water, and weight in one account.
2. The strongest landing-page line is "Fitness, precisely tracked."
3. The visual hook is the actual iPhone diary, with violet nutrition numbers on an ink background.
4. Show the native iPhone food picker, serving quantity, save action, and resulting diary totals.
5. Twenty-one seconds gives the logging sequence and result clear holds.
6. Polished, direct product footage. Keep the phone large; let the interaction explain the product.
7. Warm rhythmic music, a soft reveal, and a quiet click; no narration.
8. "Pick a meal, set your portion, and see your day add up. Exerly, on your phone."
9. Open Lunch → choose a recent meal → set 1.5 servings → save → see the updated diary. These are actual simulator actions against the isolated real API.

## Visual identity

Background #0a0a0f, foreground #e9ebf1, violet #8b5cf6 and #a78bfa. Inter matches the landing page. Keep the native app's actual SF typography inside the phone recording. Large headlines remain readable at mobile playback sizes. Small labels identify this as an iPhone app preview using demo data. Do not imply App Store availability.
