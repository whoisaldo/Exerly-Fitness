Beginner food logging and setup

Scan a barcode directly from the diary or while building a meal. Search food is beside Scan; label scanning and manual creation are in More food options. A camera match opens the portion review before anything is logged.

First-time setup now explains the app and asks about training experience, weekly workouts, equipment and food preferences. It keeps your answers when interrupted, shows starting Calorie and macro targets, and explains what those estimates mean. U.S. units remain the default. Your explicit metric choice is preserved.

Workout plan builder

Build a plan from your goal, experience, available time and equipment. Saved setup fills your weekly schedule, experience and equipment. Review the workout days and the reasons for the plan before saving. Saving works offline, keeps your current program, and can be undone in Suggestions. Choose Follow program separately when ready.

Please try the first setup, scan a packaged food, review its portion, and build a workout plan. Physical camera, VoiceOver, Watch and heart-rate checks are still pending. This build does not claim full MacroFactor parity.
