Build a meal with several foods, review its combined Calories and macros, adjust each portion, and log everything together.

Choose Add food > Build a meal. Add saved foods or search the database. Added foods have a check, and Review meal brings you back to the portions and totals. Nothing enters the diary until you tap Log meal.

Portions default to U.S. ounces and fluid ounces where supported. Exact quantities survive editing, offline logging and relaunch. Unknown nutrients stay unknown.

The meal review uses Exerly's purple and pink identity, with more room for food names and larger text. Cancelling a meal asks before discarding your changes.
