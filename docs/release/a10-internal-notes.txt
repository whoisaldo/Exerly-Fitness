Exerly 1.0: U.S. food portions

New foods default to ounces, or U.S. fluid ounces when a recorded volume basis is available. Choose grams, milliliters or a named serving from the portion measure sheet. Existing explicit portions and metric account preferences keep their measures.

Switching measures preserves the exact weight and nutrients, including fractional servings. Saved units survive offline logging and relaunch. Estimated weight from volume is labeled, and foods without a density do not offer volume conversion. Quick portion presets now show their selected value. Calories remain kcal and macros remain grams.

Try changing a food from ounces to grams and to a named serving, then back. Save offline, reopen and reconnect. The previous build's per-entry nutrition corrections and scrolling fix are included.

Enable Tailscale for devbox1 staging and use the existing internal account. Nutrition label scanning is still being tested and is not in this build. Physical-device permission, VoiceOver and performance checks and full feature parity remain open.
