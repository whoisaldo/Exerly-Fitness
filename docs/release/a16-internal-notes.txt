Faster portion choices and clearer food entry

Food entry now opens with Calories and macros above the amount controls. The header shows the selected meal and day. More detailed nutrition and corrections remain below the meal choices.

When a food provides a label serving, choose it directly with a button such as Use 1 bar or Use 1/2 cup. Each button selects one of that exact supplied portion. You can still adjust the quantity before logging. Ounces and U.S. fluid ounces remain the defaults; explicit metric preferences and previously saved portions stay as entered. Serving weights display in ounces for U.S. accounts, without rounding stored data.

Please try choosing a label portion, cancelling it, then logging offline and reopening the entry. Known zero and missing nutrients stay distinct. Quick Calories and macros, saved gyms, guided setup, barcode-first logging and the workout builder are included. Physical camera, VoiceOver and Watch checks remain pending.
