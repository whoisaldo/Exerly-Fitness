Faster food logging and workout guidance

Quick add logs Calories and macros without creating a food or entering a weight. You can correct the entry after an offline relaunch. Missing nutrients remain distinct from known zero.

Food entry now puts Calories and macros above the amount controls. Supplied label portions have direct choices such as Use 1 bar. Ounces and U.S. fluid ounces remain the defaults; your saved portions and metric preferences stay as entered.

Seven common movements now have offline setup, numbered steps and a control cue. Open How to do it from your workout and return to the same sets and rest timer. Largest-text rest controls now stay readable and can extend or skip the timer.

Saved gyms let you choose equipment for your training plan. Exercise search keeps Close available while you browse. Guided setup, barcode-first logging and the workout builder are included.

Please try logging a food portion offline, using Quick add, and opening a guide during a rest. The written exercise guides cover seven movements; broader coverage and demonstrations are still in progress. Physical camera, VoiceOver and Watch checks remain pending.
