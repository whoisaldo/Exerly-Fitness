## External release evidence

These gates require evidence in addition to code. They are not complete until the named check has run.

- Xcode 26 or later and a platform 26 SDK for App Store uploads. Local Xcode 16.4 remains useful for compatibility testing. See [Apple's upload requirements](https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/).
- Signed physical-iPhone tests for camera packages, HealthKit, notification delivery, permissions, backgrounding and thermal behavior.
- Staging provider entitlement and live-response checks, with credentials kept outside the repository.
- Nutrition bounds and supported-population review by a qualified professional before public automated weight-loss advice.
- Repository protection and required checks, staging deployment, backup restore, alert delivery, deletion and rollback rehearsals.
- At least 14 days and 1,000 beta sessions for the plan's crash-free-session gate. Record the sample and dates; do not substitute synthetic sessions.
